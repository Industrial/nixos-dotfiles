# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-01

- DungeonJournal opens from the rows EasyFind already has: click a boss to open the journal on its page (Alt still lists its loot in the bar), Alt-click an item to open the journal on the boss that drops it with the item highlighted (a recipe on the journal's recipe page), and right-click a dungeon to open it or its map in the journal.
- Open in DungeonJournal in the right-click menu of every such row, and of the Questie extension's quest rows for the journal's dungeon quests.
- Without the Questie extension, the journal's dungeon quests are rows of their own: level, dungeon and state, a click opens the quest's page, Shift-click links it in chat, a GPS button to the quest giver; switches for turned-in, other-faction and other-class quests.
- The boss's level and the item's every source from the journal under the rows' tooltips.
- A DungeonJournal tile in the extensions menu, with the journal's options in its right-click menu.
