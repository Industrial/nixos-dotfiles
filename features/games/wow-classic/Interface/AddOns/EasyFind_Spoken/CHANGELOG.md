# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-31

- Right-click a quest, zone, subzone, dungeon, boss or letter in EasyFind's search results and open its Spoken flyout to hear Spoken's line for it: Play in Spoken, and Open in Compendium for its page in Azeroth's Compendium.
- Quests come from EasyFind's Questie extension, letters and books from your bags, and zones, subzones, dungeons, raids and bosses from EasyFind's own search (and its Dungeon Journal extension's rows, if you use it).
- What Spoken keeps until you find it stays greyed out, with the reason on hover, and so does a line no installed voice pack has yet.
- Spoken's Unlock switches, the same ones on Spoken's own page, sit right under a greyed Play in the flyout and on the Spoken page of EasyFind's Extensions tab.
- While a line plays, its entry reads Stop.
- Type "stop spoken" in the search bar for Stop all Spoken narration: it ends every line Spoken is reading or has waiting and closes its narrator.
- A boss's story is heard when Spoken's Places and Bosses voice pack has it, and read in Spoken's narrator when it does not.
- Open in Compendium closes the search bar as the Compendium opens.
- On Classic Era and Anniversary, Spoken's map button sits against the map window's edge, a side tab's place, instead of floating beside it. With EasyFind's side panel open it moves out with the window.
- Needs EasyFind 4.1.0 or newer; zones, subzones, dungeons and letters need Spoken 3.7.0 or newer. Classic Era, Anniversary and WoW Forever.
