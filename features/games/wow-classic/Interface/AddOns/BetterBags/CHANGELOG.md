Adds a searchable in-game Help to the settings window and fixes backpack items dimming at the guild bank.

## New

- **Searchable in-game Help in the settings window**: A new Help page in the settings window offers 80 searchable articles on everything BetterBags does, complete with links and the settings themselves. Open it with /bb help followed by optional search words, or find articles under Help Articles in settings search.

## Fixed

- **Backpack items no longer dim at the guild bank**: Backpack items are no longer dimmed while the guild bank is open. Previously, every item in your backpack, including tradable inks that the guild bank accepts, was greyed out during a guild bank visit.
