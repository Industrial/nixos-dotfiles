# ModernMapMarkers - Custom Map Credits

This addon features custom high-resolution dungeon map illustrations created by **Santiago Reyes** for the *World of Warcraft: Forever* community ("Atlas de Azeroth: Forever" project).

## Terms of Use & Proclamation Compliance
* **Non-Commercial:** In accordance with the creator's proclamation, these maps are provided free of charge and are never used in connection with any monetization, commercial sales, or services prohibited by Blizzard's EULA.
* **Integrity of Art:** All original map watermarks and artist credits are intentionally preserved on the illustrations.
* **Authorization:** Used with official community authorization under the guidelines set forth by the artist for the Forever community projects.
