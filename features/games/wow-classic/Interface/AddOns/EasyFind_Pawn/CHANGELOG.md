# Changelog

All notable changes to EasyFind: Pawn Extension are documented in this file.

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-17

### Added
- **Pawn's verdict on every gear row**: search any item, a boss or a dungeon, and gear Pawn rates above what you wear shows how much better it is and for which scale after the row's text: a boss drop, something in your bags, a crafted piece or a vendor's
- **Hover** a rated row for what the item replaces and the other scales it helps; **right-click** it to compare the item in Pawn or open Pawn
- **@upgrades ranked by Pawn**: under each gear slot, the drops Pawn rates as upgrades for the character you play, biggest first. Other characters' pages keep EasyFind's own comparison
- **The Pawn tile** in EasyFind's extensions menu opens @upgrades
